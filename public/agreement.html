The deposit is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances. By paying the deposit, the Client secures Wilson Creative Co.'s time and resources, which cannot be recovered upon cancellation.

If the Client cancels the project after work has commenced:

  • The deposit is forfeited in full — this is the kill fee

Wilson Creative Co. reserves the right to cancel the agreement if the Client is unresponsive for more than 14 consecutive business days. In this event, the deposit is forfeited and no further obligation exists on either party.
