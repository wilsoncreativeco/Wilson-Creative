/ About Us

Where ritual meets craft.

Born in Brisbane, NOIR is a space for slow mornings, soft light, and good energy. Coffee, matcha, and fresh pastries — simple, done right. Nothing overworked, nothing forced. Just quality you can feel, every time.

Clean design, natural textures, and a calm rhythm that lets you switch off for a moment. Settle in, take your time, and let the morning unfold.
